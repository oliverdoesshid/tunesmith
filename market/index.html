TuneSmith did not open? One-click links target the tunesmith:// protocol. If TuneSmith does not open, download the preset and its .sig file and place both in %APPDATA%\TuneSmith\presets\community, then restart TuneSmith. Keeping the .sig preserves the publisher's signature, but presets copied here load as unverified because they have no signed catalog entry.

Each entry carries a detached Ed25519 signature over the exact preset bytes and a SHA-256 digest. Signatures are verified against TuneSmith's pinned publisher key before publication.
